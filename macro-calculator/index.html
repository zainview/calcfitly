Understanding Macros

The building blocks of every diet.

🧮 How Is It Calculated?

First, we calculate your daily calorie target using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (adjusted for your goal). Then we split those calories into macros:

  • Protein: 4 kcal per gram
  • Carbs: 4 kcal per gram
  • Fat: 9 kcal per gram

✅ Tips for Hitting Your Macros

  • Plan your protein first — it's the hardest macro to hit.
  • Use a food tracking app to log meals for the first few weeks.
  • Prep meals in advance so you're never guessing.
  • Don't stress about being perfect — aim within 5–10g of each target.
  • Re-calculate your macros every 4–6 weeks as your weight changes.
FAQs

Macro Questions, Answered

Quick answers to common macro questions.

What are macros?

Macros (macronutrients) are the three main nutrients your body needs in large amounts: protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Each provides calories — protein and carbs have 4 kcal per gram, fat has 9 kcal per gram.

How much protein do I need per day?

For active people, 1.6–2.2 grams per kg of body weight is a common recommendation. Our calculator sets protein based on your goal — higher for fat loss and muscle gain to protect muscle mass.

What macro split is best for weight loss?

A higher-protein split like 40% protein, 30% carbs, 30% fat works well for most people. Protein keeps you full and preserves muscle while you lose fat.

Do I need to hit my macros exactly every day?

No. Aim to be within about 5–10 grams of each target. Consistency over weeks matters far more than perfection on any single day.

Is my data saved when I use this calculator?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. Your details and results are never stored or sent anywhere.
