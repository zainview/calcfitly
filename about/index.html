Our Mission

CalcFitly was created with one simple goal: to make health and fitness calculations free, fast, and accessible for everyone — no sign-ups, no downloads, no hidden fees.

What We Offer

  • BMI Calculator — Check your Body Mass Index instantly
  • Calorie Calculators — Find your daily calorie needs
  • Macro & Nutrition Tools — Plan your diet with precision
  • Body Fat & Fitness Tools — Track your progress

Why Trust Our Calculators?

All our calculators are built on established, peer-reviewed scientific formulas — including the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calories, the WHO standards for BMI, and the U.S. Navy method for body fat estimation.

We regularly review and update our tools to reflect the latest health research and guidelines.

Our Values

  • Free forever — Health tools should be accessible to all
  • Privacy first — Your data never leaves your browser
  • Accuracy — Science-backed formulas, clearly explained
  • Simplicity — No clutter, just answers

Important Note

CalcFitly provides educational tools only. Our calculators are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your doctor or a qualified health provider with any questions about your health.
