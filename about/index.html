Why We Built This

The story behind your favorite health tools.

🚫 Tired of Paywalls & Sign-ups

Most health websites hide basic calculators behind registrations, subscriptions, or endless ads. We think that's wrong. Knowing your BMI or daily calorie needs shouldn't cost you money — or your personal data.

🔬 Science-Backed, Always

Every CalcFitly tool is built on established, peer-reviewed formulas:

  • BMI: World Health Organization (WHO) standards
  • Calories: Mifflin-St Jeor equation (Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics)
  • Body Fat: U.S. Navy circumference method

No made-up formulas, no gimmicks — just real science you can trust.

🔒 Privacy by Design

Here's something unique: all calculations happen entirely in your browser. Your weight, height, age, and results never touch our servers. We couldn't see your data even if we wanted to — and we don't want to.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

CalcFitly provides educational tools only. Our calculators are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making decisions about your health, diet, or exercise routine.
