What Is BMR? Basal Metabolic Rate Explained + How to Calculate

Your body burns calories even while you sleep — that number is your BMR. Here's what it means, how to calculate it, and how to use it for weight loss.

📅 Sep 25, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read ✍️ CalcFitly Team
In this guide: What is BMR? How to calculate your BMR BMR vs TDEE — what's the difference? How to use BMR for weight loss Can you increase your BMR? FAQs

Ever wondered why some people seem to eat a lot and never gain weight? The answer often lies in three letters: BMR.

Understanding your BMR is the foundation of every smart diet plan. In fact, when we explained how many calories you should eat a day, BMR was the starting point of that entire calculation. Let's break it down in simple words.

What Is BMR?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest — just to keep you alive. Think of it as your body's "idle mode" energy cost.

Even if you stayed in bed all day doing absolutely nothing, your body would still burn calories to:

  • ❤️ Keep your heart beating
  • 🫁 Keep your lungs breathing
  • 🧠 Power your brain
  • 🌡️ Maintain your body temperature
  • 🔧 Repair cells and run hundreds of internal processes

Here's the surprising part: your BMR accounts for 60–75% of all the calories you burn each day. Yes — most of your daily calorie burn happens without any exercise at all!

How to Calculate Your BMR

The most accurate and widely used method is the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, recommended by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics:

Mifflin-St Jeor Formula — Men
BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5
Mifflin-St Jeor Formula — Women
BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161
🧮 Example: 30-Year-Old Man
Stats: 75 kg, 175 cm, 30 years old
Step 1: 10 × 75 = 750
Step 2: 6.25 × 175 = 1,093.75
Step 3: 5 × 30 = 150
Step 4: 750 + 1,093.75 − 150 + 5
✅ BMR = 1,699 calories per day (at complete rest)

BMR vs TDEE — What's the Difference?

This is where most people get confused, so let's make it crystal clear:

BMR TDEE
Full form Basal Metabolic Rate Total Daily Energy Expenditure
What it includes Only rest calories (breathing, heartbeat, etc.) BMR + walking + exercise + digestion + daily activities
Size Smaller number Always bigger (BMR × activity factor)
Use it for Understanding your metabolism Planning weight loss or gain 🎯

Simple rule: BMR is what you burn doing nothing. TDEE is what you actually burn living your life. For diet planning, TDEE is the number that matters — you can find yours with our TDEE calculator.

How to Use BMR for Weight Loss

Here's the safe, science-backed approach — and one big mistake to avoid:

❌ The Big Mistake: Eating Below Your BMR

Many people think "eat less than BMR = faster weight loss." This backfires. Eating below your BMR for long periods can cause:

  • Slower metabolism (your body adapts and burns less)
  • Muscle loss — which lowers your BMR even further
  • Fatigue, hair loss, and nutrient deficiencies
  • Rebound weight gain when you return to normal eating

✅ The Right Way

  1. Calculate your BMR with our BMR calculator
  2. Find your TDEE — multiply BMR by your activity level (or use the TDEE calculator)
  3. Eat 300–500 calories below your TDEE — never below your BMR
  4. Keep protein high to protect muscle — check your target with our protein calculator

This approach gives you steady fat loss of about 0.5 kg per week — without destroying your metabolism. For a complete breakdown, read our guide on how many calories you should eat a day.

Can You Increase Your BMR?

Yes — to a degree. Here's what actually works:

  • Build muscle 🏋️ — Muscle burns roughly 3x more calories at rest than fat. Strength training 2–4 times a week is the #1 way to raise your BMR long-term.
  • Eat enough protein 🍗 — Protein has a higher thermic effect (your body burns more calories digesting it) and protects muscle during weight loss.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours 😴 — Poor sleep disrupts hunger hormones and slows metabolism.
  • Stay hydrated 💧 — Even mild dehydration can slightly reduce metabolic rate. Our water intake calculator can help you find your daily target.
  • Avoid crash diets 🚫 — Severe calorie restriction is the fastest way to lower your BMR.

The Bottom Line

Your BMR is your body's baseline — the calories you burn just by existing. Know it, respect it, and never eat below it for long. Combine it with your TDEE for smart diet planning, and build muscle to keep your metabolism strong for life. 💚

⚠️ A quick note: BMR formulas are estimates accurate for most people within about 10%. If you have a thyroid condition or a long dieting history, consult a healthcare professional for personalized guidance.
