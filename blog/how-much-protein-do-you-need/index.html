How Much Protein Do You Need a Day? (By Weight & Goal)

From couch potato to muscle building — your exact daily protein target in grams, explained simply.

📅 Oct 2, 2026 ⏱️ 8 min read ✍️ CalcFitly Team
In this guide: Why protein matters so much The simple g/kg formula Protein needs by body weight What 100 g of protein looks like Does protein timing matter? FAQs

Protein is the one nutrient everyone argues about. Your gym buddy says you need 200 grams. Official guidelines say 50. Who's right? Both — for different people. Let's find your number. 💪

Why Protein Matters So Much

Protein isn't just for bodybuilders. It's the building material for:

  • 💪 Muscle: Repair and growth after every workout (and every daily movement)
  • 🦴 Bones, skin, hair: All made largely of protein
  • 🛡️ Immune system: Antibodies are proteins
  • ⚖️ Hunger control: Protein is the most filling macronutrient — a secret weapon for weight loss
  • 🔥 Metabolism: Digesting protein burns more calories than carbs or fat (the thermic effect)

Eat too little, and your body quietly breaks down muscle to get what it needs — especially during weight loss.

The Simple g/kg Formula

Forget percentages. The easiest science-backed method uses your body weight:

Your Lifestyle Protein Per kg Body Weight
🪑 Sedentary (official minimum) 0.8 g / kg
🚶 Active (regular exercise) 1.2 – 1.6 g / kg
🏋️ Muscle building 1.6 – 2.2 g / kg
📉 Weight loss (muscle protection) 1.2 – 1.6 g / kg
👴 Over 50 (muscle preservation) 1.0 – 1.2 g / kg
🧮 Quick Example: 70 kg Person
Sedentary: 70 × 0.8 = 56 g/day
Active: 70 × 1.4 = ~98 g/day
Building muscle: 70 × 1.8 = ~126 g/day
✅ Same body, three goals — protein triples from minimum to muscle building.

What 100 g of Protein Looks Like

Numbers on paper are easy — plates are harder. Here's roughly how common foods stack up:

Food Serving Protein
🍗 Chicken breast 100 g cooked ~31 g
🥚 Eggs 2 large ~12 g
🐟 Fish (salmon/tuna) 100 g ~22–25 g
🧀 Paneer 100 g ~18 g
🥣 Greek yogurt 170 g ~17 g
🫘 Lentils (dal) 1 cup cooked ~18 g
🥜 Peanuts 30 g handful ~8 g

Vegetarian? Totally doable — combine dal, paneer, yogurt, tofu, chickpeas, and eggs across the day. Aim ~10% higher than the table since plant proteins digest slightly less efficiently. And remember: protein fits inside your daily calorie budget — our macro calculator balances all three macros for you.

Does Protein Timing Matter?

Good news: the famous "30-minute anabolic window" is mostly gym mythology. Research shows:

  • 📊 Total daily intake matters most — hitting your grams beats perfect timing
  • 🍽️ Spread it out: 25–40 g per meal across 3–4 meals beats one giant protein bomb
  • ⏰ Post-workout window is hours wide — eating within 2–3 hours of training is plenty

So relax — no need to sprint to your shaker bottle mid-workout. 😄

The Bottom Line

Minimum to survive: 0.8 g/kg. To thrive, stay full, and build muscle: 1.2–2.2 g/kg depending on your goal. Calculate your number, hit it daily with foods you enjoy, and let consistency do the rest. 💚

⚠️ A quick note: People with kidney disease should follow their doctor's protein limits. For healthy adults, intakes up to 2.2 g/kg are considered safe in current research.
