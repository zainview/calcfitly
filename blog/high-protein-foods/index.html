The Budget Protein Champions 💰

Protein doesn't need to be expensive. Ranked by cost efficiency (most protein per rupee/dollar):

  1. Lentils/dal — the undisputed champion. A kg of dry dal yields ~225g protein for pocket change
  2. Eggs — complete protein, endlessly versatile, cheap everywhere
  3. Canned tuna — 30g protein per can, shelf-stable
  4. Milk — 8g per glass plus calcium
  5. Peanuts & peanut butter — protein + healthy fats
  6. Chicken thighs — nearly half the price of breast, same protein
  7. Chickpeas/rajma — buy dry, soak overnight, cook in bulk

Money-saving combo: Dal + rice isn't just comfort food — together they form a complete amino acid profile. Traditional food wisdom was right all along.

Complete vs Incomplete Proteins (Simplified)

Proteins are built from 20 amino acids; 9 are "essential" (your body can't make them):

  • Complete proteins (all 9 essentials): meat, fish, eggs, dairy, soy (tofu/tempeh/edamame), quinoa
  • Incomplete proteins (missing 1-2): lentils, beans, nuts, grains

Do vegetarians need to panic? No. You don't need complete protein at every meal — just variety across the day. Dal at lunch + yogurt as a snack + paneer at dinner covers everything. Your body pools amino acids over 24 hours.

A Sample 130g Protein Day (Real Food, No Stress)

5 Tips to Hit Your Target Every Single Day

  1. Anchor every meal with protein first. Decide the protein (chicken? paneer? eggs?), then build the plate around it. Never the other way round.
  2. Front-load your day. 30-40g at breakfast makes the rest of the day easy. Skipping protein early = playing catch-up till midnight.
  3. Double your dal. One katori of dal = 9g. Two katoris = 18g. The simplest upgrade in any Indian kitchen.
  4. Keep emergency protein ready. Boiled eggs, roasted chana, Greek yogurt, canned tuna — zero-cooking options save you on busy days.
  5. Track for just one week. Most people overestimate their protein intake by 30-40%. Seven days of honest tracking recalibrates your eye forever. Our macronutrient guide shows how protein fits your full calorie picture, and the calorie counting guide makes tracking painless.

The Bottom Line

Protein isn't complicated — it's a list, a target, and a habit. Pick 3-4 sources you actually enjoy from each category, anchor every meal with one of them, and your daily target handles itself. Screenshot these tables. Your future grocery trips just got a whole lot smarter.
