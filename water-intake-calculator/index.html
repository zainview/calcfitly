Why Hydration Matters

Your body is about 60% water — keep it topped up.

🧮 How Is It Calculated?

Our calculator uses a widely recommended base formula:

Base intake = your weight (kg) × 33 ml

Then we adjust for your lifestyle:

  • Exercise: +350 ml for every 30 minutes of activity
  • Hot climate: +500–750 ml (you lose more through sweat)
  • Pregnancy/breastfeeding/illness: +300–700 ml extra

✅ Signs You're Well Hydrated

  • Pale yellow or clear urine
  • You rarely feel thirsty
  • Good energy levels throughout the day
  • Skin feels elastic and healthy

⚠️ Signs of Dehydration

  • Dark yellow urine or strong odor
  • Dry mouth, lips, or headaches
  • Feeling tired or dizzy
  • Difficulty concentrating

Pro tip: Don't wait until you're thirsty — thirst is already an early sign of dehydration. Sip water regularly throughout the day.

FAQs

Hydration Questions, Answered

Quick answers to common water questions.

How much water should I drink a day?

A common guideline is about 33 ml per kg of body weight. For a 70 kg person, that's roughly 2.3 liters per day. Exercise and hot weather increase your needs.

Does coffee or tea count toward my water intake?

Yes, mostly. While caffeine has a mild diuretic effect, the water in coffee and tea still contributes to your daily hydration. Plain water is still the best choice.

Can I drink too much water?

Yes, though it's rare. Drinking extreme amounts in a short time can cause hyponatremia (low sodium). Spread your intake throughout the day and listen to your body.

How much extra water should I drink when exercising?

A good rule is to add about 350–500 ml for every 30 minutes of moderate exercise, and more in hot conditions or during intense workouts.

Is my data saved when I use this calculator?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. Your weight and results are never stored or sent anywhere.
