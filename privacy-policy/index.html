Privacy Policy

How we handle your information — explained in simple, honest language.

Last updated: September 2026

🌟 Good news: All CalcFitly calculators run entirely in your browser. Your health data never leaves your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to CalcFitly ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit calcfitly.com (the "Site").

By using our Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Site.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

CalcFitly is designed with privacy at its core. We do not collect or store:

  • Your height, weight, age, or any health data you enter into our calculators
  • Your name, email address, or contact details (unless you voluntarily email us)
  • Any calculation results — everything is processed locally in your browser
  • Passwords or account credentials (we don't have accounts!)

3. Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, some non-personal information may be collected automatically when you visit:

  • Log data: Browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent (collected by our hosting provider, Cloudflare)
  • Cookies: Small files used for site functionality and analytics (see Section 5)

This information is anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our Site. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit www.aboutads.info/choices to opt out of third-party vendor cookies.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them for:

  • Essential functions: Making the Site work properly
  • Analytics: Understanding how visitors use the Site (anonymously)
  • Advertising: Displaying relevant ads through Google AdSense

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. The Site will still work, though some features may be limited.

6. Contact Form

If you contact us through our contact form or email (contact@calcfitly.com), we will receive your name, email address, and message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and never share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data Security

Our Site is served over HTTPS (secure encrypted connection) via Cloudflare. Since we don't store personal health data, there is no database of user information that could be compromised.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will promptly address it.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: contact@calcfitly.com
  • Contact Form: calcfitly.com/contact
