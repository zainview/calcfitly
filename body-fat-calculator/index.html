Understanding Body Fat

What your percentage actually means.

📏 How to Measure Correctly

  • Neck: Measure just below the Adam's apple, tape level and relaxed.
  • Waist: Measure horizontally at your belly button. Stand relaxed — don't suck in!
  • Hip (women only): Measure around the widest part of your hips/buttocks.
  • Take measurements in the morning before eating for the most consistent results.

🧮 The U.S. Navy Method

Developed by the U.S. Navy, this method estimates body fat using simple circumference measurements. It's accurate within about 3–4% for most people — making it one of the best at-home methods without expensive equipment like DEXA scans.

Why it beats BMI: BMI only compares weight to height, so a muscular athlete can be labeled "overweight." Body fat percentage measures actual fat — a much better picture of your health.

FAQs

Body Fat Questions, Answered

Quick answers to common questions.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

For men, 14–24% is generally considered healthy. For women, 21–31% is healthy. Athletes typically have lower ranges: 6–13% for men and 14–20% for women.

How accurate is the U.S. Navy body fat method?

The Navy method is accurate within about 3–4% for most people when measurements are taken correctly. It's one of the best at-home methods without special equipment.

How do I measure my waist correctly?

Measure horizontally around your abdomen at the level of your belly button, while standing relaxed. Don't suck in your stomach or pull the tape too tight.

Is body fat percentage better than BMI?

Body fat percentage is often more useful because it measures actual fat, while BMI only compares weight to height. A muscular person can have a high BMI but low body fat.

Is my data saved when I use this calculator?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. Your measurements and results are never stored or sent anywhere.
