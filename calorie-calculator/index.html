Understanding Your Calories

The science behind your daily energy needs.

🧮 How Is It Calculated?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — recommended by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics as the most accurate BMR formula:

Men: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age + 5

Women: BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age − 161

Your BMR is then multiplied by your activity level to get your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) — the calories you actually burn each day.

⚖️ Safe Weight Loss Tips

  • Aim for a 500 calorie/day deficit — that's about 0.5 kg loss per week.
  • Never eat below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) without medical supervision.
  • Combine a small calorie deficit with regular exercise for best results.
  • Prioritize protein to preserve muscle while losing fat.
  • Slow, steady progress beats crash diets every time.
FAQs

Calorie Questions, Answered

Quick answers to common calorie questions.

How many calories should I eat per day?

It depends on your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. On average, adult women need 1,600–2,400 calories and adult men need 2,000–3,000 calories per day. Use our calculator for a personalized estimate.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the calories your body burns at complete rest. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is your BMR multiplied by your activity level — it's the total calories you burn each day.

How many calories should I cut to lose weight?

A deficit of about 500 calories per day leads to roughly 0.5 kg (1 lb) of weight loss per week, which is considered safe and sustainable. Never go below 1,200 calories (women) or 1,500 calories (men) without medical supervision.

Which formula does this calculator use?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is considered the most accurate BMR formula by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics.

Is my data saved when I use this calculator?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser. Your age, weight, height, and results are never stored or sent anywhere.
